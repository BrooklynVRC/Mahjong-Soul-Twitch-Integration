1. Catch a banner

When a banner appears in game (RIICHI, RON, TSUMO, results…), click Freeze. You don't need to be fast: it keeps the last 20 seconds. Slide to the clearest frame, then drag a box tightly around the banner's word.

2. Save it as a template

Tips for good templates

• Box only the part that looks the same every time, like the banner's word. Leave out tiles, avatars and the table.
• Opponent banners appear on their side: teach one per side and give them all the same event.
• Banners animate, so pick the frame where the word is sharpest and fully visible.
• Matching uses brightness, so colour changes don't matter much.
• After saving, click Scan frozen frames below to check the score jumps on the banner frames only.
