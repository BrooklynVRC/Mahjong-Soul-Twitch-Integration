1. Catch something on screen

When a banner appears (RIICHI, RON, TSUMO, results…) click Freeze. It keeps the last 20 seconds, so there's no rush. Slide to the clearest frame and drag a box. For scores, freeze any moment where the score panel is visible.

Live

Tips

• Box only the part that looks the same every time, like the banner's word.
• Easiest: teach one "Riichi / Ron / Tsumo (anyone)" banner each and teach the score boxes. The reader then works out who it was from the score change.
• Or teach per-side banners (e.g. "My riichi" on your side, "Opponent riichi" on each other side).
• Game mode: box the room/mode name shown when a match loads and name the template after it, e.g. "Gold Room · East".
• After saving, use Scan frozen frames to check the score jumps on the banner frames only.

3. Banner templates

The bar shows how closely the current picture matches each template; the yellow mark is the threshold. Raise it if it fires by mistake, lower it if it misses. Templates for the same event share one cooldown. Templates and score boxes are shared by the Twitch and Lovense apps.
